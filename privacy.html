Privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Eclipse Studio operates Twin Trigger Outbreak. You can play without creating an account.

Online play

The game’s online services store your chosen player name, game level, a randomly generated device-specific player identity, a friend code, friends, friend requests, and invites in a database hosted by Supabase. These records let the game recognize returning devices and connect friends. Other players can see your display name and game level where they are used in friends and multiplayer features.

To connect internet co-op devices directly, the game uses Google and Cloudflare network discovery servers to learn the public IP address and port. The online service exchanges connection details, including local network addresses where needed, with the players joining the lobby. This data supports connection setup and lobby operation. The game has no voice or text chat.

Purchases and future features

Apple processes purchases when the in-game shop is enabled. The game receives purchase confirmations to deliver items and restore eligible purchases. Eclipse Studio does not receive your payment card details. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Optional Sign in with Apple and rewarded video ads through Google AdMob are planned. The current build does not use them. We will update this policy and the App Store privacy information before making them available.

Retention and your choices

We keep online identity and friends records to support returning players. Lobby connection details are used while routing online games. You can remove friends in the game or ask us to access, correct, or delete online data through the support page. We may verify control of the associated game identity before processing a request. Please ask for a private follow-up before sharing any identifying details.

Support requests

Support uses GitHub Issues, where requests are public. GitHub handles information you provide there under its privacy statement.
